1. Introduction & Core Architecture

PeerLink is engineered from the ground up on a Zero-Cloud, Zero-Tracking, Peer-to-Peer (P2P) architecture. Unlike conventional chat, calling, and file sharing applications, PeerLink operates without centralized cloud servers, remote databases, analytics intermediaries, or telemetry collection.

Fundamental Privacy Guarantee: Your messages, calls, media files, and prompts never touch our servers because we operate no servers. All communications travel directly between peer devices over local Wi-Fi, Wi-Fi Direct, or Hotspot connections using authenticated end-to-end encryption.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

PeerLink does not collect, record, harvest, transmit, broker, or sell any of the following:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): No phone numbers, email addresses, real names, or national identification credentials.
  • No Cloud Account Profiles: The app requires no user account, registration, login, or cloud credential.
  • No Location Tracking or GPS Logs: The application does not collect, log, or transmit device coordinates.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics SDKs: There are no analytics libraries (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Adjust, AppsFlyer) embedded in the application.
  • No Advertising IDs: No Google Advertising ID (AAID) tracking or behavioral profiling.

3. Android System Permissions & Data Usage

PeerLink requests only permissions strictly necessary to execute user-initiated offline features. Here is how each permission is handled:

Camera (android.permission.CAMERA)
Used strictly in real time for peer-to-peer video calling and scanning device QR codes for out-of-band cryptographic pairing. Video camera frames are streamed directly to the connected peer node over encrypted local sockets. No video feeds or photos are captured or transmitted to third parties.
Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO)
Used solely for real-time voice and video calls between connected peers, and user-initiated local call recording. Audio is encoded locally (Opus/PCM), encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and sent directly to the recipient peer. Call recordings are saved solely into user-accessible local storage (Downloads/PeerLink).
Storage & Downloads Access (READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)
Used solely to read user-selected files for P2P transmission, save received files transferred by peers into the public Downloads/PeerLink directory, retain local chat history in SQLite/Room, and save locally downloaded GGUF AI models for offline neural inference.
Local Network & Nearby Devices (NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN)
Used to broadcast and discover nearby devices using standard zero-configuration networking (mDNS/DNS-SD service name _peerlink._tcp), Wi-Fi Direct, and Bluetooth Low Energy beacons. Used exclusively for local subnet socket negotiation.
Screen Capture & MediaProjection (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION)
Used exclusively when the user manually initiates screen sharing with a peer. Video frames of the mirrored screen are encoded locally and piped directly to the authenticated peer. Display mirroring immediately terminates when stopped by the user or when the call ends.
Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS)
Used strictly on-device to notify you of incoming audio/video calls, incoming file transfers, and when a previously chatted peer reconnects to the local mesh.
Shizuku Privileged System Access (Optional)
Used strictly on-device if authorized by the user via the Shizuku API for Wi-Fi scan throttling management, low-latency Wi-Fi mode, and socket diagnostics. It does not extract, read, or export personal data.

4. Cryptography & End-to-End Encryption

Every direct peer-to-peer connection is secured using modern cryptographic standards:

  • Authenticated Encryption: AES-256-GCM with 128-bit authentication tags ensures confidentiality and tamper prevention.
  • Key Negotiation: Elliptic-Curve Diffie-Hellman (ECDH) on Curve secp256r1 negotiates unique ephemeral session keys per peer connection.
  • Zero-Knowledge Identity: Cryptographic key fingerprints are derived locally from public keys; private keys never leave device hardware.
  • Replay Protection: Monotonic packet sequencing and timestamp validation prevent packet injection and replay attacks.

5. Offline AI & Local Neural Engine

PeerLink includes a local inference engine supporting on-device quantized neural models (such as Qwen GGUF). When you interact with the Offline AI engine:

  • All prompt tokens, context windows, and model generations are processed 100% locally on your device CPU/GPU.
  • Zero prompts, queries, or AI responses are uploaded to external APIs, cloud servers, or third-party AI providers.
  • Chat histories with the offline assistant remain stored solely in your local app sandbox.

6. Data Retention & User Control

You retain complete and exclusive ownership of your data at all times:

  • Local Storage: All chat threads and metadata reside exclusively within the on-device SQLite database managed by Android Room.
  • Instant Thread Deletion: Deleting a conversation immediately purges all associated messages from the local SQLite database.
  • Complete App Wipe: Clearing app storage in Android System Settings or uninstalling the app permanently and irreversibly erases all keys, messages, and configurations.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

PeerLink does not collect or transmit personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. Because the application operates on an offline peer-to-peer model without central servers or data harvesting, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy frameworks.

8. Voluntary Rewarded Advertising (Google Mobile Ads)

PeerLink includes optional, user-initiated rewarded video ads provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to support development and grant temporary Turbo Boost throughput. Viewing rewarded ads is completely voluntary and is never required for core offline P2P communication, encrypted chats, or local AI inference. When interacting with rewarded ads, Google Mobile Ads may process standard advertising identifiers and diagnostic signals in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play Developer Program policies.
